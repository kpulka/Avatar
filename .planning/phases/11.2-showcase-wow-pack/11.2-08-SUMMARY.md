# Phase 11.2 Plan 08: Bilabial closure Summary

M/B/P now close the lips with jaw ~0, lip press and lip roll plus a low `mouthClose`, instead of a high `mouthClose` that drags the MetaPerson lower lip toward the nose. Jaw left over from the previous vowel is suppressed by the press amount too.

## Delivered

- `pose()` gains `rollLower` / `rollUpper` (`mouthRollLower` / `mouthRollUpper`; both are in the normaliser and test-avatar shape lists).
- P/B/M table pose: `jaw 0.02, close 0.5, press 0.58, rollLower 0.25, rollUpper 0.15`.
- `viseme-scheduler.ts`: `PRESS_REF = 0.35`; the jaw suppression gate and ratio use `closure = min(1, max(activeMouthClose / closeScale, activePress / PRESS_REF))`.
- Tests: effective-pose test for M/B/P under balanced (computed through `VisemeScheduler`, converged hold), cap check for all three presets, jaw-suppression test (vowel jaw 0.4 overlapping M ends < 0.05; verified the test fails when `PRESS_REF` is neutralised).

## Deviations from Plan

1. **[Rule 1 - consequence] `tests/viseme-overrides.test.ts` edited (outside files_modified).** One assertion hard-coded the old table value (`defaultVisemeShapes('M').get('mouthClose') === 0.8`); changed to 0.5. Direct consequence of the mandated table change; nothing skipped or loosened.
2. **Table press is 0.58, not 0.35 as in the plan example.** `scaleForShape` multiplies `mouthPress*` by `min(closeScale, 0.85)` exactly as it does `mouthClose` (0.60 balanced), so the plan's "press unscaled" assumption was wrong; 0.58 x 0.60 = 0.348 meets the plan's effective target (0.35 +- 0.05).
3. **`activePress` is accumulated from the effective `targets` value** of `mouthPressLeft` (same way as `activeMouthClose`, i.e. already scaled and eased) rather than from `item.shapes * blendWeight`, so `pressRatio` compares like with like against `PRESS_REF` (the effective press). The jaw-suppression gate also became `activeMouthClose > 0.15 || activePress > 0.15`.
